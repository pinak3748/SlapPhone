## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 App preferences and sound data

- Selected sound packs, favorites, and in-app sound preferences

- History of which features you use within the app (e.g., sound categories), where stored locally or as described below

- In-app activity such as screens visited and features used (may be aggregated or anonymized)

- **Motion sensor data** may be processed on your device in real time to enable slap / motion-based sound effects. We do not use this to identify you personally.

- App version  
- Crash reports and error logs (to improve stability)  

We do **not** collect your name, email, or personal identifiers unless you contact us for support.

### 2.4 Information you provide

- Preferences and settings you save in the app  
- Notification preferences  
- Any information you share when contacting our support team (e.g., email content)

- **Legal requirements:** If required by law, court order, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of our users or the public.  
- **Business transfers:** In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your data may be transferred to the successor entity.
